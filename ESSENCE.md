We consider the project to be on the performing phase. We think the project team meets every requirement to be seeded, since we have established
rules and responsibilities on the first session. We do not check every item on the formed section, since we are 3 and the team sizes are supposed to be
between 4 and 5. As a team we are having in real life lab sessions and open communication through agreed upon channels, so we meet the Collaborating stage checklist.
The Performing checklist on the other hand might be a bit tricky, since we consider we are not the most optimally working team, but we do work independently
and good enough to check most of the items. Finally, since the deadline has not yet ended we will not check the last items, hoping to change this after presentation.
